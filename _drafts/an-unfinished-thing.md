Anything in `_drafts/` is invisible on the real site. No date is needed in the
filename here.

To see drafts while you're working locally:

```sh
bundle exec jekyll serve --drafts
```

When it's ready, move the file into `_posts/` and rename it with today's date
in front: `2026-09-01-an-unfinished-thing.md`.
